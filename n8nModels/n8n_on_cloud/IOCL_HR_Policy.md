## 1. Purpose and Scope

This manual sets out the human resources policies for sales personnel of Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL, dummy) working in the Marketing Division. It covers employees who sell petrol (MS), diesel (HSD) and lubricants to business customers, including logistics fleets, manufacturers, power and infrastructure companies, mining and agriculture firms, government and public transport bodies, and hospitality and retail chains.

The policy applies to employees in the Direct Sales (Industrial and Commercial), Retail Outlets Bulk Supply, Lubricants Marketing, and Institutional and Government Sales divisions. Where this manual and a written employment contract differ, the contract prevails.

## 3. Working Hours and Attendance

Office-based working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with alternate Saturdays working. Field sales staff may start and end their day at customer sites but must log visits in the sales app by 7:00 PM each day.

- Employees must mark attendance daily in the HR portal, or log a customer visit when working in the field.
- Late arrival of more than 30 minutes, three times in a month, counts as one half-day of casual leave.
- Work on a weekly off or public holiday requires prior manager approval and earns compensatory leave.

## 5. Compensation and Sales Incentives

Pay has a fixed component and a variable incentive linked to the annual B2B sales target set for each employee. Targets are expressed in Rs crore of revenue and are reviewed each April.

| Designation                             | Fixed pay share | Variable (incentive) share |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------- |
| Sales Officer / Senior Sales Officer    | 85%             | 15%                        |
| Territory Manager / Key Account Manager | 80%             | 20%                        |
| Area Sales Manager                      | 75%             | 25%                        |
| Regional Sales Manager                  | 70%             | 30%                        |

### 5.1 Incentive slabs

The variable component is paid according to target achievement, measured as revenue achieved divided by the pro-rata annual target for the period.

| Target achievement | Incentive payout (% of variable pay) |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Below 80%          | 0%                                   |
| 80% to 99%         | 50%                                  |
| 100% to 119%       | 100%                                 |
| 120% and above     | 125% (maximum cap)                   |

- Incentives are calculated quarterly and paid within 30 days of quarter-end.
- Revenue is counted only when the customer invoice is raised and the payment terms are within policy (see Section 7).
- A product-mix bonus of 2% of the incentive applies when lubricants account for at least 10% of an employee's quarterly revenue.
- Incentives are withheld during any open disciplinary investigation.

## 8. Code of Conduct and Ethics

All employees must act with integrity in dealings with customers, dealers, suppliers and government bodies.

- **Gifts and hospitality:** gifts above Rs 2,500 in value, or any cash gift, must be declined or reported to HR within 7 days.
- **Conflict of interest:** employees may not hold a financial interest in a customer, dealer or transporter without written disclosure.
- **Anti-bribery:** payments or favours to influence tenders, government approvals or inspections are strictly prohibited.
- **Confidentiality:** customer pricing, volumes and contract terms are confidential and may not be shared outside the company.
- **Respectful workplace:** harassment or discrimination of any kind is prohibited. Complaints go to the Internal Committee (Section 11).

## 9. Health, Safety and Environment

Petroleum products are hazardous. Anyone visiting a terminal, depot, retail outlet or customer storage site must follow site safety rules.

- Wear the prescribed PPE (safety shoes, helmet and high-visibility jacket) in all operational areas.
- No mobile phone use, smoking or naked flames within the marked hazardous zone of a fuel facility.
- Report any spill, fire, near-miss or injury to the HSE officer and your manager within 2 hours.
- Mandatory fire safety and first-aid training is repeated every 24 months for all sales staff.

## 10. Performance Review and Promotion

Performance is reviewed twice a year (April and October) against sales targets, customer retention, collection discipline, safety record and behaviour.

| Rating                | Description                                   | Typical outcome                               |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| A - Outstanding       | Consistently above target with strong conduct | Eligible for promotion and top increment band |
| B - Exceeds           | Meets or beats most goals                     | Above-average increment                       |
| C - Meets             | Meets core goals                              | Standard increment                            |
| D - Needs improvement | Below target or conduct concerns              | Performance improvement plan for 90 days      |

Promotion to the next grade normally needs a minimum of 3 years in the current grade, two consecutive ratings of B or higher, and a successful interview panel.

## 11. Grievance Redressal and Disciplinary Action

Employees can raise concerns informally with their manager or formally through the HR portal. HR acknowledges every grievance within 3 working days and aims to close it within 21 days. Complaints of sexual harassment go to the Internal Committee, which follows the statutory process and keeps all details confidential.

Misconduct may lead to a verbal warning, written warning, suspension, withholding of increments, demotion or termination, depending on severity. Serious offences such as fraud, bribery or safety violations may result in immediate suspension pending enquiry.
