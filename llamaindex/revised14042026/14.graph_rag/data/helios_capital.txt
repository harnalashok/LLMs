# Investment Dossier: Helios Capital Management

Helios Capital is a venture capital and private equity firm based in New York City, specializing in deep tech, quantum infrastructure, and autonomous systems. Helios is managing partner for the $1.2B "Frontier Tech Fund IV".

## Leadership & Key Investors
- Victoria Sterling: Managing Partner and Founder of Helios Capital. Sterling sits on the advisory board of the National Science Innovation Council.
- Julian Mercer: Principal Investment Director. Mercer oversees quantum and neuromorphic investments. He serves on the board of directors of Aurora Technologies and Aether Labs.
- Sofia Chen: Chief Legal Counsel. Sofia previously represented Synapse Dynamics in its Series B fundraising round.

## Portfolio & Financial Interconnections
Helios Capital is the lead financial backer for multiple intersecting enterprises:
1. Aurora Technologies: Helios invested $30 million in Series B and holds a 15% equity stake.
2. Aether Labs: Joint venture between Helios Capital and Aurora Technologies established in September 2023.
3. Synapse Dynamics: Helios acquired a 22% minority stake in Synapse Dynamics in November 2023 for $60 million.
4. Project Nexus: An unannounced cross-institutional initiative funded covertly by Helios Capital to merge quantum compression chips with autonomous humanoid robotics.

Confidential internal correspondence shows that Julian Mercer orchestrated the technology transfer between Aurora Technologies, Aether Labs, and Synapse Dynamics.
